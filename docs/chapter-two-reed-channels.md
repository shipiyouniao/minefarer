# Chapter Two: the ferry investigation

## Regional arc

The lead is the rift at the old western ferry. The traveler wants a way home; Nia wants to establish what happened to the river before exposing anyone else to it. Water moving in separate, repeatable pulses suggests control rather than weather. Tidekeeper is the planned chapter boss family; the later confrontation and its motives remain to be scripted and implemented.

The chapter plan is five exploration stages followed by one boss stage. Reed Channels is exploration stage one. Subsequent stages should investigate the inner cove and its keeper, the mooring controls, the upstream pulse source and the protected crossing, with an independent optional river-side rescue. These are planning subjects, not shipped entrances. The regional ending must resolve safe passage and advance the homeward lead instead of sending the player on another report-and-return errand. Human pacing estimates remain unmeasured.

## Delivered first stage

After Reedbank's arrival exchange, a once-only conversation notices the reversed current and accepts the main task Why the Ferry Stopped. Recollection remains optional: its ignition is not a prerequisite for this main stage. The physical drainage marker at pier cell 50 enters Reed Channels; the task points to the camp on the atlas, which also shows that landmark.

Topology and device positions were authored first. Mine placements were selected offline against a public-clue solver and frozen as explicit content rows; runtime never generates these layouts from a fixed seed. The tests exercise deductions and visible circuit labels, never hidden mine positions, and complete without tool use or damage. Solid banks and levees are authored terrain boundaries, separate from generated dungeon walls. Switching a sluice also advances a predictable tide: the connected A/B bank stays fixed and disconnected lanes rotate one tile along their displayed arrows. Whole tile records (including flags and visibility) travel together and every clue is recomputed. The two banks contain one, two and three four-tile lanes respectively across the three floors; later floors use opposing directions. Player, tile discoveries and tile-bound resources follow the same permutation; stale geometric sonar/scan reports are cleared. Gateways and controls stay fixed. A board-side guide explains the interaction, and tide movement is animated.

The ending establishes that the intact gates stop at repeatable marks and locates the moored ferry in the inner cove. It awards 160 shared supplies once and records `ferry-channel-cleared`, completing the main task on site. After clearance, pier cell 50 becomes a doorway to the playable Old Ferry scene, with a matching return doorway. Both board and atlas show the destination instead of the retired stage marker. The next substantial stage is not implemented in this increment. The old mine remains independent of this task and chapter.

`reed-channels-v2` owns a new campaign slot; previous chapter revisions and Recollection journals are untouched. Every accepted action can be replayed after reload. Interrupted endings are recovered after settlement without re-awarding currency. Existing equipment, skills and ordinary task/achievement counters apply. The stage combines reversible routing with deterministic tidal tile movement. Obsolete v1 attempts are retired instead of replayed under new rules; cleared history and rewards remain, and previously cleared players receive the onward road. Recollection eligibility for this combined mechanic remains later work.
